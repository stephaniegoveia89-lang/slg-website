Does this sound familiar?

Growth changes how a business needs to run.

In the early days, being close to everything is an advantage. You know the clients, the projects, the finances and every decision.

But at some point, the business gets bigger than the systems holding it together. In a digital business, that shows up fast: projects grow beyond their scope, the team is stretched across too much work at once, and it's hard to tell which clients and projects actually make money.

You haven't outgrown your business. You've outgrown the way you've been running it.
  • 01

    Everything still runs through you

    Decisions, approvals and problems still land on your desk, and managers aren't sure where their authority starts and stops.

  • 02

    You're growing, but the financial picture is blurry

    Your reports tell you what happened, but not what's coming. It's hard to know where you really stand.

  • 03

    Strong team, but you're still pushing everything

    You've hired well, yet you're still the one driving every priority forward.

  • 04

    Things start, but don't finish

    Projects compete for the same people, new opportunities are hard to assess, and good ideas lose steam.

  • 05

    You need senior leadership, not another full-time hire

    The business needs an experienced operator, but you're not ready to add a full-time executive.

What changes

Build a business that doesn't depend on you holding it all together.

The goal isn't more meetings, more paperwork or another layer of management. It's an organization where people know what matters, have the information and authority to act, and can deliver without every decision landing on your desk.

  • 01

    Financial Clarity and Control

    Know where the business stands, where it's heading and what's driving results. Build forecasting you can trust, tighten financial processes and protect your margins.

  • 02

    Operational Structure That Scales

    Put in the processes, systems and workflows that let you grow without adding needless complexity. Clear the bottlenecks and use your team's capacity well.

  • 03

    Leadership and Accountability

    Set clear roles, stronger management habits and a meeting rhythm that keeps people aligned. Your team takes ownership instead of waiting on you.

  • 04

    Strategy Into Action

    Turn priorities into plans people deliver. Get better at weighing opportunities, assigning resources and finishing what you start.

  • 05

    Better Performance, Not Just Smoother Operations

    When you understand how your people, processes, capacity and finances fit together, you can make better calls on where to invest, what to prioritize and how to grow profitably.

How I work

Not another advisor telling you what to change. A partner who helps make it happen.

Plenty of consultants can assess a business and hand you a list of recommendations. The hard part is putting them in place.

I embed in your business. I work directly with you and your leadership team to understand how things actually run, find what's getting in the way and put practical fixes in place. Sometimes that's financial forecasting. Sometimes it's reshaping responsibilities, improving how projects get delivered, setting up management reporting or helping your team adopt new technology. Often it's a mix.

I don't bring a ready-made framework your business has to squeeze into. I work with your people, your resources and your reality to build something that lasts.

An embedded partner, not an outside advisor

A traditional consultant studies your business from the outside. As your fractional Chief of Staff, I become part of it, and that changes what's possible to fix.

The Outside Advisor

  • Delivers a report or slide deck, then moves on to the next client.
  • Learns your org chart, rarely your organization.
  • Applies the same framework used with every other client.
  • Looks at processes one at a time, without seeing how people, finances and decisions connect.
  • Recommends, then leaves you to implement and troubleshoot alone.
  • Sees one moment in time, then disappears.

The SLG Approach

  • I stay embedded until the new way of working is running, accountable for the outcome, not just the recommendation.
  • I learn your people, relationships and sensitivities, the things that never show up on an org chart.
  • Everything is built from what's actually happening inside your business, not a template pulled off the shelf.
  • I connect the dots between operations and money, so you see how decisions affect revenue, margins and capacity.
  • Implementation, change management and follow-through are all part of the work, so changes stick.
  • I'm close enough, long enough, to catch shifts like a new hire or a changed priority, and adjust before they become problems.
